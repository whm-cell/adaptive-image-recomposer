# Radial and Network

Use this family when centrality or verified connections can replace a linear reading order.

## Kernels

- `focal-orbit`: one central thesis and directional satellites.
- `constellation-network`: distributed clusters connected only where relationships are known.
- `radial-wedges`: unequal content territories around a focal center.
- `hub-spoke-dashboard`: compact summary hub with asymmetrical detail spokes.

## Failure controls

- Do not encode unverified magnitude through orbit distance, wedge size, or spoke length.
- Route dense copy to attached rails instead of forcing it around circles.
- Reject decorative networks that do not establish a reading path.
- Avoid a generic dashboard look unless the paired visual system explicitly calls for it.
