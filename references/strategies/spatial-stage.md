# Spatial Stage

Use this family when objects should feel exhibited or staged in depth while information remains auditable.

## Kernels

- `museum-exhibit-wall`: one hero exhibit, satellites, and caption rails.
- `scene-shelf`: foreground, middle, and background shelf layers with a data rail.
- `isometric-stage`: restrained platforms with front-facing copy.
- `depth-gallery`: a shallow perspective sequence of near, middle, and far frames.

## Failure controls

- Keep important copy flat and readable; never typeset required text in deep perspective.
- Shadows may unify semantic objects but must not alter locked pixels.
- Reject a scene that buries comparison data inside props or scenery.
- Do not invent physical product dimensions or packaging details.
