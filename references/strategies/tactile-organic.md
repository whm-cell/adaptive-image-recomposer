# Tactile and Organic

Use this family when irregular material territories can replace rigid containers and create a markedly different silhouette.

## Kernels

- `cut-paper-collage`: a dominant fragment followed by smaller cutouts.
- `liquid-islands`: interlocking fluid territories separated by whitespace channels.
- `botanical-archipelago`: organic islands connected by restrained botanical gestures.
- `textile-patchwork`: variable patches linked by seams or stitches.

## Failure controls

- Reject decorative blobs placed behind the original layout.
- Keep material texture away from small exact copy.
- Botanical or wellness motifs must not imply medical efficacy or unsupported natural claims.
- Controlled irregularity still requires an explicit reading path.
