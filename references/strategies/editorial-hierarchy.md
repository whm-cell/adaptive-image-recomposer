# Editorial Hierarchy

Use publication grammar when the source needs a strong thesis, uneven scale, and a deliberate mix of leads, columns, annotations, or pull facts.

## Kernels

- `feature-cover-stack`: oversized lead with overlapping secondary features.
- `newspaper-spread`: lead story, variable columns, rules, and pull facts.
- `split-feature-dialogue`: two unequal fields connected by a bridge element.
- `annotated-column-essay`: a main narrative column with controlled marginal detours.

## Failure controls

- Reject equal cards disguised as editorial modules.
- Do not reproduce a source's fifty-fifty split or original column count.
- Keep dense copy horizontal, legible, and attached to its semantic node.
- An editorial hierarchy may vary emphasis but must not imply a factual ranking unless one exists.
